About Me

I'm a computational engineer passionate about building systems that accelerate scientific research. Currently at St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, I lead HPC infrastructure initiatives and serve as the institutional resource for AI Agents across all HPC clusters.

My work spans from designing large-scale monitoring infrastructure with Prometheus and Grafana to architecting MLOps-focused Python packages that enable researchers to train machine learning models in a low-code fashion. I've expanded our Open OnDemand instance from a single cluster to a multi-cluster deployment spanning four environments.

Originally from Jordan, I graduated magna cum laude from Rhodes College and am currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at UT Austin, focusing on parallel systems and deep learning.
